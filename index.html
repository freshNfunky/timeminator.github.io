Core Features

Live Timer + Manual Booking

Flexible input methods: start/stop timer for real-time tracking or manually log hours. Custom categories (Client Project, Research, Internal) let teams allocate time exactly how they work.

Budget vs. Actual Analytics

Three curves per project: overlay your plan against actuals in one view. Pro-employee breakdowns show time allocation across project types, catching imbalance early.

Team Sentiment Tracking

Monthly sentiment ratings (personal & project-level) combined with workload data surface burnout risk, staffing gaps, and customer satisfaction in one loop.

Why Timeminator, Not SaaS

No bloat. Invoicing, expense reports, mileage tracking removed. You get time tracking, budget analytics, and team sentiment. That's it.
Custom categories. Define your own project types (Kunde / Forschung / Intern). Most SaaS force generic "projects" + "tags."
Budget insights. Three-curve view (Budget / Time / Resources) is unique to Timeminator. Toggl and Clockify have zero budget integration.
Self-hosted. Run locally on SQLite (zero infrastructure) or on your own MySQL server. No vendor lock-in, no SaaS bills, full GDPR control.
Sentiment tracking. Combine workload data with team mood. No other time tracker does this out of the box.
Audit-ready by design. Immutable booking history, versioned imports, role-based permissions. Built for teams that need proof.
